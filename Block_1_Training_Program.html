Day B — Lower Quad Dominant (Quads, Glutes, Calves)

Day B — Lower Quad Dominant

~70 min • Monitor back
⚠ If lower back flares on squats in Weeks 1–2, swap to goblet squat or hack squat machine instead. Never push through spinal pain.
#ExerciseSets × RepsRestCues
1
Back Squat (High Bar) — or Hack Squat if pain
Primary compound		 4 × 6–8
W1: 3RIR		 2–3 min
Brace 360° before descent. Knees track toes. At or below parallel. Drive knees out on ascent. If lower back aches after set, drop to hack squat.
2
Leg Press (feet high and wide)
Secondary compound		 3 × 10–12 2 min
High foot placement shifts load to glutes. Don't let lower back curl off pad at bottom. Control descent.
3
Bulgarian Split Squat (Dumbbells)
Unilateral		 3 × 10 each 90 sec
Rear foot elevated. Front shin stays vertical. Feel the stretch through the front glute. No spinal compression — safe for back.
4
Leg Extension (Machine)
Isolation		 3 × 15 60 sec
2-sec hold at top. Toes slightly inward for VMO. Full extension, no hyperextension.
5
Standing Calf Raise
Isolation		 4 × 20 45 sec
Full stretch at bottom (1 sec). Full squeeze at top (1 sec). No bouncing.
6
Dead Bug (Core rehab)
Core — back rehab		 3 × 10 each side 60 sec
Lower back FLAT on floor throughout. Breathe out as you extend arm + opposite leg. Slow and controlled. This is medicine for your back.
🏃
Post-session: Bike (preferred over treadmill) — 15 min, easy pace, protects lumbar

Day C — Upper Pull

Day D — Lower Posterior (Glutes, Hamstrings) — Deadlift-Free

Day D — Posterior Chain (No Deadlifts)

~65 min • Back-safe build
✅ This session has been fully redesigned to build your posterior chain without any spinal loading under bar. Hip thrust is the primary. All exercises are lumbar-safe. Deadlifts re-introduced in Block 2 once cleared.
#ExerciseSets × RepsRestCues
1
Barbell Hip Thrust
Primary compound — glutes		 4 × 8–10
W1: 3RIR		 2 min
Upper back on bench at shoulder blade height. Bar padded, sits across hip crease. Drive through heels. Posterior pelvic tilt at TOP — tuck pelvis, squeeze glutes hard for 2 sec. Chin tucked, don't hyperextend neck. ZERO lumbar stress when done correctly.
2
Seated Leg Curl (Machine)
Hamstrings		 4 × 10–12 90 sec
Seated gives more hamstring stretch than lying. Flex foot at the bottom. 3-sec eccentric. Both hamstring heads get hit — much safer than RDL right now.
3
Cable Pull-Through
Hip hinge — rehab progression		 3 × 15 90 sec
Rope between legs from low cable. Push hips back (NOT bend forward), feel hamstring stretch. Drive hips forward to stand. Pure hip hinge — teaches the pattern safely. Load is pulling horizontally, NOT compressing spine. This is your deadlift substitute right now.
4
Single-Leg Romanian Deadlift (Dumbbell, light)
Rehab progression — Weeks 3+ only		 3 × 10 each — skip W1-2 90 sec
START WEEKS 3–4 ONLY if pain-free. Single-leg version teaches hip hinge with minimal lumbar load. Hold one light DB. Hinge at hip, keep spine neutral. Touch DB to floor, drive standing leg's hip forward. Don't progress load until pain-free for 2 full weeks.
5
Seated Calf Raise
Soleus		 4 × 20 45 sec
Seated targets soleus (deep calf). Full ROM. 1-sec pause at stretch and at squeeze.
6
Bird-Dog (Core / Lumbar rehab)
Core — back rehab		 3 × 10 each side 60 sec
On hands and knees. Extend opposite arm and leg simultaneously. Don't rotate hips or arch low back. Hold 2 sec at extension. One of the most evidence-backed exercises for lumbar rehab. Do it slowly — this is not for tempo, it's for motor control.
🏃
Post-session: Bike only (no stairmaster) — 15 min easy — protect the lumbar on Day D

Movement posture quick reference

Bench Press Elbows flaring >90° → shoulder impingement over time
Squat Knee cave (valgus collapse) on ascent → knee injury risk
Hip Thrust Hyperextending lumbar at top instead of glute squeeze → lower back stress
Pull-Up Shrugging up instead of depressing scapula → no lat activation
Cable Pull-Through Bending spine forward instead of hinging at hip → defeats purpose
Bird-Dog Rotating hips or arching back on extension → compensation, not rehab
