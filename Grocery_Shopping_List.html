Meal prep tip — Sunday and Wednesday: Cook 1kg of rice, 800g chicken breast, and roast a tray of sweet potato and veg in one go. Portion into containers. Having 4–5 meals ready to go eliminates decision fatigue and stops you reaching for garbage food when you're tired after work. This single habit accounts for about 80% of nutritional success.
Budget hack: Buy chicken thighs instead of breast sometimes — 30% cheaper, slightly higher fat but still high protein, more flavourful. Frozen fish is nutritionally identical to fresh. Frozen veg (broccoli, spinach) retains micronutrients as well or better than "fresh" that's been sitting in a truck for 4 days.
